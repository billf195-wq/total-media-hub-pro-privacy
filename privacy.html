Total Media Hub Pro — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 4, 2026

Total Media Hub Pro is a free TV-style home screen for your PC. This policy explains, in plain words, what happens with your information.

Your information stays on your PC

  • Your tiles, profiles ("Who's watching"), pictures, calendar reminders, weather places and settings are saved only on your PC.
  • Pictures you add for profiles or tiles stay on your PC. They are never uploaded, shared or included in any file the app sends anywhere.
  • The language you pick for the app's screens is a setting saved on your PC.
  • The app has no account, no sign-in of its own, no advertising and no analytics or tracking.
  • The app does not collect, sell or share your personal information.

Weather

  • To show weather, you type a US ZIP code. The app looks up that ZIP code's location from a list built into the app.
  • The app then asks the US National Weather Service (api.weather.gov) for the forecast and any weather alerts for that location. That request includes the location (latitude and longitude) of the ZIP code you typed.
  • The app never uses your device's location (GPS, Wi-Fi location or similar).
  • You can turn the weather off in Settings at any time.

Phone remote (optional, off until you turn it on)

  • If you turn on the phone remote, the app listens only on your home network (local Wi-Fi). It does not accept connections from the internet.
  • A phone must enter a PIN shown on your TV before it can control the app. Paired phones are remembered on your PC only, and you can remove them in Settings.

Websites and apps you open

  • Total Media Hub Pro opens websites (for example streaming services) in your web browser, and opens apps already installed on your PC.
  • Those websites and apps have their own privacy policies. Anything you do there (signing in, watching, buying) is between you and that service.
  • Total Media Hub Pro is not affiliated with any of those services. You need your own subscriptions and accounts.

Tips (optional)

  • The app is free. The optional "Send a tip" button opens PayPal in your web browser.
  • PayPal handles the payment and your payment details under PayPal's own privacy policy. The app never sees your payment details.
  • A tip does not unlock anything.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
